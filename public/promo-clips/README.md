## How to Import into Synthesia
1. Log into your **Synthesia account** and open your **LocalEats SA** video project.
2. In the media panel, click **Upload Media** (or **Add Asset**).
3. Select the 6 downloaded `.webm` files from this package.
4. Drag each clip into its corresponding scene on the video timeline.
5. In Synthesia, set the media playback mode to **Fit to Background** or **Centered Video Showcase**.
6. The clips will play silently at 30 fps while your existing avatar or voiceover delivers the script flawlessly.
